## Cover formulas that work

- Name the pain: "Your regulars walk out. You never learn their names."
- Command + number: "Stop typing these 5 WhatsApp messages."
- Myth: "~~Loyalty cards~~ are dead. Here's what replaced them."
- Outcome in time: "Live in 10 minutes."
- Question owners ask themselves: "Who is your best customer?"

8 words or fewer. A tension or a result, never a topic label like "Tapmore features".
