## Tapmore (SaaS) — tapmore.in

- What: one QR code page for cafés, shops, salons, clinics and every local business.
- On the page: menu or price list (photos, offers, "From" prices, today's specials), Wi-Fi
  card (one-tap copy, optional email capture), digital stamp card (staff stamp from any
  phone), private feedback box, Google review invitations, birthday rewards with email,
  refer-a-friend rewards.
- For the owner: customer list with CSV export, scan analytics per QR spot, Google review
  replies in the owner's name (4–5★ can auto-reply, 1–3★ wait for approval), staff till
  login, own colours/logo/fonts with no Tapmore branding on the page.
- Setup: answer four questions (name, type, colour, reward); add menu by typing or by
  photographing a printed menu ("we'll read it for you"); print QR cards at home. "Set it up
  yourself in ten minutes."
- Guests need no app: scan with the phone camera, works on slow 4G.
- Change anything after printing: the same QR always shows the latest version.
- Price: **NOT FIXED YET — never put a price (₹999, ₹3 a day or any other) on slides or in
  captions** until the owner says the price is final. Use the trial instead: **7-day free
  trial, no card needed.** If you stop paying, the page pauses and comes back exactly as it was.
- Demos on tapmore.in: Juniper Coffee House, Honeycomb Café, Bloom Bakery, Glow Salon,
  Sharma General Store (these are demo pages, not customers — never call them customers).
